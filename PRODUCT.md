## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is church leadership and administration: Vicars, Church Secretaries, Trustees, Church Office Staff, and other church administrators. They evaluate and use Church Govern to coordinate church operations, maintain trusted information, and serve their communities.

Church Members are also an important user group, particularly through the Member Suite, where they engage with relevant church information and services.

Editorial teams manage approved public content through Sanity Studio. Authorized operational staff use the separate administration console for leads, moderation, audit, and aggregate analytics.

## Product Purpose

Church Govern is a comprehensive, connected church administration and engagement platform. It brings together church office administration, member engagement, records, finances, events, communication, documents, and digitization through complementary Office Suite and Member Suite experiences.

The product exists to make church operations easier to understand, coordinate, and sustain while giving members a considered path to relevant church services. Success means church teams can work from more connected information and clearer workflows without losing the human responsibility and context behind church administration.

## Positioning

Church Govern connects the administrative work of church offices with the member-facing experience rather than treating them as unrelated systems. Its Office Suite and Member Suite share a coherent foundation across records, workflows, communication, documents, finances, events, and responsible digitization while presenting role-appropriate experiences to each user group.

The platform is denomination-neutral and intended to be suitable for any church.

## Operating Context

Church operations may span physical registers, historical records, spreadsheets, documents, inboxes, phone calls, office visits, finance processes, events, member requests, and recurring administrative workflows. Church Govern is evaluated through a public marketing website and product demonstration before implementation details and approved client content are finalized.

Sanity is the editorial system of record for pages, product modules, blogs, FAQs, navigation, media, and SEO. Supabase stores operational data such as leads, comments, ratings, topic suggestions, audit events, and aggregate analytics. The private operational dashboard is distinct from the editorial CMS.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Office Suite and Member Suite with 17 defined product modules.
- Public pages for Home, About, Product, module details, Blogs, FAQ, Contact, Security and Compliance, and required legal information.
- Structured editorial content, media, navigation, and SEO managed through Sanity.
- Enquiry and digitization workflows, operational notifications, comments, ratings, topic suggestions, moderation, audit, and aggregate analytics supported through server-validated operational services.
- Public content should be rendered server-side or statically where practical; private operations require authenticated, role-checked access.
- Mock content may demonstrate complete structure during development but must remain clearly labelled and cannot enter production.
- Approved content must be preserved unless the owner explicitly requests a change.
- Do not introduce unapproved customer names, testimonials, pricing, compliance certifications, security claims, statistics, company history, product screenshots, or other factual claims.
- Final production ownership, approved content and media, exact security and legal language, retention policies, analytics accounts, and deployment acceptance remain external approval gates.

## Product Principles

1. Connect church administration and member engagement through one coherent foundation.
2. Keep experiences role-appropriate while preserving shared information and accountability.
3. Respect denomination neutrality, church context, continuity, privacy, and responsible stewardship.
4. Make provisional, approved, and unavailable information unmistakably different.
5. Preserve product truth: demonstrate structure and capability without inventing proof or guarantees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website and operational interfaces must be responsive, keyboard-operable, semantically structured, compatible with reduced-motion preferences, and usable with visible focus and labelled errors. Denomination-sensitive language must remain neutral and inclusive. Formal WCAG acceptance ownership and the final cross-browser, screen-reader, zoom, and reflow audit remain launch requirements.
